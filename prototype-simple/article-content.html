Upgrading your home is supposed to be a joyful milestone. Whether you are moving to a luxury villa or a compact studio, the excitement is often quickly followed by a strange sense of dread.

This hesitation is not mere procrastination. Medical science confirms that moving triggers a distinct, measurable physiological response in the body.

Heading 2 The Clinical Reality of Relocation Stress

Relocation Stress Syndrome was formally recognized as a clinical diagnosis by the North American Academy of Psychiatry. Studies by researchers Kao, Travis, and Acton (2004) demonstrated that moving frequently triggers measurable psychological strain.
