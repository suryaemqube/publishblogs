What Swan checks

  • Every paragraph carries a named style
    Structure is read from styles only. Direct bold and manual font sizes are never guessed at, because guessing is what puts a heading in the middle of a paragraph.
  • Each article starts with a boundary style
    A Title, a Heading 1, or a Swan Label opens a new article. Without one, Swan cannot tell where the previous article ended.
  • No heading jumps a level
    H1 to H3 directly breaks the outline and the on-page navigation.
  • No empty paragraphs used as spacers
    Use paragraph spacing. Stray blanks become stray breaks on the live site.
  • Internal links point at a real target
    Checked inside this document and against the sites this article will ship to.
A file that fails any of these is rejected, not repaired. The Swan-Article-Template.docx download below is the fastest fix, and Swan links to the exact paragraph that broke so nobody has to hunt for it.
